Privacy Policy

Minnvaan is an offline video and music player. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your data.

01 What Minnvaan accesses

Minnvaan asks for permission to read the videos and audio already stored on your device so it can list and play them. It does not need or request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

02 What stays on your device

Everything Minnvaan remembers — playback positions, playback speed and zoom choices, chapters and bookmarks, favorites, recently played history, your chosen accent color, and private folder settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

03 Private folders & PIN

If you set a privacy PIN to hide a folder, only a one-way cryptographic hash of that PIN is stored locally. The PIN itself is never saved in readable form and is never sent anywhere.

04 Network access

Minnvaan requests internet access solely so you can optionally play a video from a URL you type in yourself (the "Open from URL" option). The app does not otherwise make network requests, run analytics, show ads, or communicate with any server.

In short: no analytics SDK, no ad network, no crash-reporting service, no background sync. If you never use "Open from URL," Minnvaan makes zero network requests.

05 Third parties

Minnvaan does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

06 Children's privacy

Minnvaan does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be reflected in the app and on this page with a new date above.
